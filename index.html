Start here

Your questions, depending on who you are.

Three people read this page for three different reasons. Pick yours.

Who is looking after my child?

Your child's own teachers, all day. They travel on the school bus with the group, stay with them in their marked zone, and do the headcount before leaving. Our volunteers help them — they never replace them.

What if my child feels unwell?

Certified doctors and an ambulance are on the ground for the whole event, with a first-aid point reachable from every zone. Tell your school about any condition — asthma, allergies, medication — and it is passed to the medical team in advance.

What time will they be home?

Buses reach the venue between 9:00 and 11:30 am. The performance is at 1:30 pm. Schools leave from 2:50 pm and all buses are away by 4:00 pm. Your school will confirm its own arrival and departure slot.

How do they get there and back?

On your school's own bus, with their own teachers. Buses park in a reserved bay. At the end, groups are called zone by zone and walked back to the same bus they arrived on.

Does my child need to be good at dancing?

No. Instructors come to the school for seven to ten days and teach the routine from zero, in short sections. Every child who attends the sessions will be able to perform it.

Do I need to send food or money?

No. Every registered student gets an event T-shirt, sealed drinking water and a packed snack. Nothing to buy or carry on the day beyond a water bottle if they prefer their own.

What exactly are we agreeing to?

Confirm participation, give an approximate student count, nominate teacher coordinators, give our trainers a slot during the training window, and arrange your own bus on the day. That is the whole commitment.

Will it disturb our timetable?

The training schedule is fixed with you, around your existing timetable. Most schools use their activity or games period. Students keep attending all regular classes through the training window.

Who carries responsibility on the day?

Your teachers supervise your students throughout — that does not transfer. We provide the ground, the zone, volunteers, medical cover, security, water and washrooms, and we run the movement plan your staff work to.

How many students can we send?

No fixed minimum or maximum. Give an approximate number when you register and your zone is sized to it. You can adjust the number until the final school list is locked.

What do we get afterwards?

A participation certificate for every student in the school's name, aerial and ground photography of the formation, and inclusion in the record documentation submitted to the adjudicators.

Why do zones fill up?

Zones are allocated in the order schools confirm, because both the training calendar and the ground map are built around the final school list. Earlier confirmation means a better position and more training slot choice.

What actually happens?

You and thousands of other students stand in your school's marked area facing a big stage, and dance the same Tollywood routine at the same time for fifteen minutes without stopping. Drones film the whole thing from above.

But I can't dance.

That is fine — most people in the formation have never done this before. Trainers come to your school and teach it step by step over a week or so. By the end you will know it well enough to do it without thinking.

What do I get?

An event T-shirt everyone wears, water and a snack box, a certificate with your name, and photos of the whole formation from the air. Plus a fairly good story.

How long is the day?

You reach the ground in the morning with your school, spend the middle of the day getting into position and warming up, perform at 1:30 pm, eat afterwards, and are back on your bus by around 3 pm.

Will my friends be with me?

Yes. Your whole school stands together in one marked zone, in rows, with your own teachers. You will not be split up or sent anywhere on your own.

How do I join?

Through your school — ask your class teacher or activity coordinator to look at this page. If your school has not registered yet, send them the link.

31 October · hour by hour

The whole day, start to finish.

Indicative timings. Your school receives its exact arrival slot, zone number and departure time on the final schedule.

9:00 – 11:30

Buses arrive

Each school arrives in its allotted slot and is directed to a reserved parking bay. Students get down with their own teachers.

11:30 – 12:15

Walk to your zone

Each school is walked along a barricaded route to its own marked section, behind a coloured flag marker, and placed row by row.

12:15 – 12:45

Warm-up

A trainer is assigned per row block. Warm-up is led from the main stage with sub-stage trainers mirroring, so the back rows see the same counts as the front.

12:45 – 13:10

Welcome & practice run

Anchors open the programme. A one-minute trial confirms every zone can hear the track and see the stage.

13:10 – 13:30

Guests arrive

Invited guests are received. Students stay in formation — no movement, no regrouping.

13:30 – 13:45

The performance

Fifteen minutes, non-stop, everyone at once. Drone and ground cameras record continuously for the record submission. It ends on a freeze pose held for five seconds.

13:45 – 14:15

Record paperwork

Attendance, participant documentation, photography and video are completed with the adjudication process.

14:15 – 14:50

Snacks and water

Distributed zone by zone so no group has to move through another. Teachers and volunteers keep each school together.

14:50 – 16:00

Headcount and home

Teachers check every student against their list. Schools are called zone by zone, walked back, and buses leave in a planned sequence.

Nothing on this day asks a child to find their own way anywhere. Students move as a school, with their school, from the bus to the zone and back again.

Safety & care

How we look after 5,000 children.

A ground with thousands of children on it is a logistics problem before it is a performance. These arrangements are staffed and in place before the first bus is let in.

Doctors & ambulance

Certified doctors and a stationed ambulance for the full duration, with a marked first-aid point reachable from every zone.

Security & crowd control

Professional bouncers and a trained volunteer team manage barricaded entry, movement routes and exit, with police permission secured in advance.

Water & washrooms

Drinking-water points around the ground, plus mobile washrooms in addition to the venue's own facilities.

Fire safety & clear routes

Fire-safety equipment on standby, marked entry and exit routes, and coordination points kept clear of student formations.

Bus-to-bus accountability

Students move school-wise, never alone. Headcount is taken before departure and every child boards the bus they arrived on.

Your teachers, throughout

School coordinators stay with their own group the entire time. Our volunteers assist them — they do not take over supervision.

1 : 1

Every student arrives with their school and leaves with their school. Schools are called zone by zone for departure and volunteers walk each group to its own bus.

Before the day

Nobody needs to know how to dance.

Our instructors come to your campus for seven to ten days and teach the routine from zero, on a schedule built around your timetable.

Learn

The routine is broken into short sections and taught one section at a time.

Practise

Repetition until it is muscle memory. Trainers correct posture and timing in place.

Synchronise

Perform

Still wondering

Questions we get asked.

Is there any cost to families?

No. The participation fee is settled between the school and our coordinator. The T-shirt, water, snack box and certificate are all included — families are not asked to pay or send anything.

My child has a medical condition. Can they still take part?

In most cases yes. Tell your school when you give consent, and they pass it to our coordinator so the medical team knows in advance. If a doctor has advised against sustained physical activity, please follow that advice.

What happens if it rains?

If the date has to change for weather or any other reason, your school is informed directly and the training schedule is re-planned with you. Please confirm the postponement and refund terms in writing with the coordinator before making any payment.

Will photos of my child be published?

The formation is photographed and filmed from the ground and the air for the record submission, which means wide shots of the crowd. Consent is collected through your school. Raise any concern about your child appearing in close-up with your school coordinator.

How is the record attempt actually verified?

Through aerial and ground video, participant attendance records and event photography. The official proceedings are completed on the ground straight after the performance, with the relevant record authorities. No record is claimed until that process is complete.

Is transport included?

No — schools arrange their own buses. We allot the arrival slot and a reserved parking bay, and volunteers guide buses in and out in a planned sequence.

How many teachers should a school send?

Your coordinator will advise based on your student count. As a guide, schools typically send one accompanying teacher per 25–30 students, plus a lead coordinator who is our point of contact on the day.

Can parents come and watch?

Arrangements for spectators are still being confirmed with the venue. Your school will be told as soon as that is settled — please do not plan on attending until it is confirmed in writing.
