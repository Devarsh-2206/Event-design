Why it matters

Why this matters

More than a dance programme.

A child who has stood in a formation of five thousand and held their count carries something afterwards that a classroom cannot hand them. That is the actual product here — the record is what makes it unforgettable.

01

A day they will describe for years

Weeks of rehearsal, then fifteen minutes in front of thousands. Most students never get a stage this size. The ones who do remember the discipline it took, not just the noise.

02

Visible on a city stage

Your students perform under your school's name alongside every other participating school in Hyderabad, with photography, video and record documentation to show for it.

03

Confidence, built in public

Fitness, timing, teamwork and the nerve to perform — taught by professional instructors on campus, at no cost to the family beyond participation.

04

One ground, many schools

Students who would never otherwise share a stage stand in the same formation, to the same count, for the same fifteen minutes.

Preparation

Before the day

Students don't need
to know how to dance.

Our instructors come to your campus for seven to ten days and teach the routine from zero, on a schedule built around your timetable rather than against it.

  1. Stage 01

    Learn

    The choreography is broken into short sections and taught one section at a time.

  2. Stage 02

    Practise

    Repetition until the movement is muscle memory. Trainers correct posture and timing in place.

  3. Stage 03

    Synchronise

    Counts, cues and transitions locked to the track so every school lands on the same beat.

  4. Stage 04

    Perform

    Full run-throughs in your school's own formation, so the day itself is a repeat, not a first time.

Event day

31 October

The run of show.

Indicative timings. Your school's exact arrival slot, zone number and departure sequence are issued on the final call sheet before the event.

09:00 – 11:30

Arrival & bus parking

Buses arrive in their allotted slot and are directed to a reserved bay. Students get down under their own teachers' supervision — no school moves without its staff.

11:30 – 12:15

Move to zone

Each school is walked to its marked section behind a coloured flag marker along barricaded routes, then placed row by row in final positions.

12:15 – 12:45

Line-up & warm-up

A trainer is assigned per row block. Warm-up is led from the main stage with sub-stage trainers mirroring, so the back rows read the same counts as the front.

12:45 – 13:10

Welcome & trial sync

Anchors open the programme and brief the ground on the attempt. A one-minute trial run confirms every zone is hearing the track and reading the stage.

13:10 – 13:30

Chief guest arrival

Invited dignitaries are received and escorted to the guest enclosure. Students hold formation throughout — no movement, no regrouping.

13:30 – 13:45

The attempt — 15 minutes, non-stop

Countdown, entry music, and the full ground performs as one. Drone and ground cameras record continuously for the record submission. The routine closes on a freeze pose held for five seconds.

13:45 – 14:15

Record proceedings

Attendance records, participant documentation, photography and videography are completed with the adjudication process for the submission.

14:15 – 14:50

Refreshments, zone by zone

Snack boxes and water are distributed section-wise so no zone has to move through another. Teachers and volunteers keep each school together.

14:50 – 16:00

Headcount & departure

Teachers complete a final headcount against their attendance list. Schools are called zone-wise, walked back along barricaded routes, and buses depart in a planned exit sequence.

Safety

Safety & event management

The part principals
ask about first.

A ground with thousands of children on it is a logistics problem before it is a performance. These arrangements are planned and staffed before the first bus is allowed in.

Doctors & ambulance on site

Certified doctors and a stationed ambulance for the full duration, with a marked first-aid point reachable from every zone.

Security & crowd management

Professional bouncers and a trained volunteer team manage barricaded entry, movement routes and exit, with police permission secured in advance.

Water & washrooms

Drinking-water stations positioned around the ground, plus mobile washrooms in addition to the venue's existing facilities.

Fire safety & clear routes

Fire-safety equipment on standby, marked entry and exit routes, and coordination points kept clear of student formations.

Bus-to-bus accountability

Students move school-wise, never individually. Headcount is taken before departure and every child boards the bus they arrived on.

Your teachers stay with your students

School coordinators remain with their own group the entire time. Our volunteers assist them — they do not replace them.

1:1

Every student arrives with their school and leaves with their school. Schools are called zone-wise for departure and volunteers walk each group to its own bus. Nothing about the day asks a child to find their own way anywhere.

Questions from schools

What principals ask us.

Do students need previous dance experience?

No. The routine is choreographed to be learnable from zero and taught in short sections over seven to ten days. Every student who attends the training sessions will be able to perform it.

Will this disturb our academic timetable?

The training schedule is fixed with your school, around your existing timetable. Most schools use their activity or games period. Students continue attending all regular classes through the training window.

How many students can we send?

There is no fixed minimum or maximum per school. Tell us your approximate number when you register and your zone is sized to it. The number can be adjusted until the final school list is locked.

Who supervises the students at the venue?

Your own teachers and coordinators, throughout. They travel with the group, stay with it in the zone, and complete the headcount before departure. Our volunteers assist with movement and positioning — they do not take over supervision.

What medical arrangements are in place?

Certified doctors and a stationed ambulance are on site for the full duration, with a marked first-aid point. Please inform your coordinator in advance about any student with a medical condition our team should know about.

Is transport to the venue included?

No — schools arrange their own buses. We allot your arrival slot and a reserved parking bay, and volunteers guide your buses in and out along a planned sequence.

How is the record attempt verified?

The performance is documented with aerial and ground-level video, participant attendance records and event photography. The official proceedings are completed on the ground immediately after the performance, with the relevant record authorities.

What happens if the event is postponed?

If the date changes for any reason, your coordinator informs your school directly and the training schedule is re-planned with you. Please confirm the postponement and refund terms in writing with our coordinator before making any payment.
