Registration and fees

Registration is completed on our booking page. The participation fee is ₹799 per student, payable on that same page at the time of registration. A school's place is confirmed once payment is received and the receipt has been sent to the organiser on WhatsApp.

Registration closes on 23 October 2026. Registrations and student numbers cannot be added or changed after that date.

The fee covers seven to ten days of in-school training by our instructors, an event T-shirt, drinking water and a snack box on the day, a school ID band, a participation certificate, event photography, and inclusion in the record documentation.

Schools arrange and pay for their own transport to and from the venue.

What the school is responsible for

  • Supervision of its own students at all times — during training, in transit, and for the whole of the event day. Our volunteers assist with movement and positioning; they do not take over supervision.
  • Obtaining consent from the parent or guardian of every participating student, including consent to being photographed and filmed.
  • Telling the coordinator in advance about any student with a medical condition, allergy or requirement our team should know about.
  • Arranging transport, and completing a headcount against its own attendance list before departure.
  • Providing accurate contact details and student numbers at registration.

What we are responsible for

  • Delivering the training programme at your campus on a schedule agreed with you.
  • Certified doctors and a stationed ambulance on site for the full duration, with marked first-aid points.
  • Security, barricaded routes, crowd management and a trained volunteer team.
  • Drinking water stations, mobile washrooms, and fire-safety equipment on standby.
  • Obtaining the permissions required to hold the event.

The record attempt

This is an attempt at a record. We do not promise, and cannot promise, that a record will be approved, awarded or recognised. No part of the fee is contingent on the outcome.

Adjudication, verification and participant documentation are carried out by the record authority and its officials, not by us. Their decision is final and outside our control.
